FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who provides industrial automation services in Pasuruan?

Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa is an industrial automation provider based in Pasuruan, East Java. We program PLCs (Siemens, Omron, Schneider Electric), design and implement SCADA systems, fabricate and wire control panels, and commission complete automated production lines. The company has operated since 2014 and entered the automation segment in 2020.

Where can I get industrial HVAC installation and maintenance in Pasuruan?

Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa handles HVAC for factories, warehouses and commercial buildings in Pasuruan and across East Java: load calculation and system design, chiller installation and integration, AHU, FCU and ducting works, chiller overhaul, refrigeration and cold storage, and preventive maintenance programs.

What services does Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa offer?

Six service areas: (1) Industrial Automation — PLC, SCADA and control panels; (2) HVAC Systems — design, chiller, AHU/FCU installation and servicing; (3) Custom Machinery — machines designed and built for a specific production line; (4) Racking Systems — selective, drive-in, heavy-duty pallet and mezzanine racking; (5) Forklift — supply and servicing of electric and LPG forklifts; (6) Repair of Industrial Parts — compressors, pumps, motors, drives and electrical panels.

Does Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa serve areas outside Pasuruan?

Yes. The team is based in Pasuruan, East Java, and serves clients across East Java and the rest of Indonesia in the manufacturing, food and beverage, power and commercial sectors. We have also worked with international companies such as Autorun Inc (Korea) and Countec Ltd.

How does a project with Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa work?

Every project follows four steps: consultation to understand requirements and site conditions; engineering design with a technical proposal, drawings and bill of materials; installation by certified technicians; and handover with commissioning, documentation, training and after-sales support. A tailored proposal is prepared within 48 hours of the first consultation.

Does Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa repair industrial machines and parts?

Yes. Our technicians diagnose and repair compressors, conveyor systems, pumps, motors and drives, hydraulic and pneumatic systems, and electrical panels. Contract maintenance programs are available for clients who want scheduled servicing all year round.

How long has Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa been operating?

Since 2014. The company started with racking systems for warehouses, expanded into industrial automation in 2020, and launched custom machine manufacturing in 2024. It now serves more than 50 clients.
