Who We Are

A Decade of Growth, Built on Trust

PT Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa was established in 2014 as a company dedicated to providing reliable industrial solutions, beginning with a strong focus on racking systems. Since our inception, we have been committed to delivering high-quality products and services that support efficient warehouse and production operations across various industries.

In line with our long-term vision and continuous development, we expanded our business portfolio in 2020 by entering the automation segment. This strategic move allowed us to support our clients in improving productivity, operational efficiency, and process reliability through modern and integrated automation solutions.

In 2024, we further strengthened our capabilities by establishing a new business segment focused on custom machine manufacturing — delivering tailor-made solutions designed to meet specific operational requirements and maximize performance. Driven by innovation and a customer-oriented approach, PT Indo Citra Jaya Sentosa continues to deliver reliable racking, automation, and custom machinery solutions that support sustainable growth and operational excellence.
